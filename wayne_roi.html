Illustrative intervention ROI

Planning-grade "low-hanging fruit" for a Detroit-profile county (CVD-led, large overdose burden)

Cardiovascular risk management at scale

$8.93B pool

CVD is a third of the burden (5,531 deaths/yr). AI-supported hypertension control + statin adherence programs target the single largest DALY pool.

Overdose prevention (naloxone + MOUD)

$4.76B pool

1,107 substance-use deaths/yr — the defining crisis. A 10% YLL reduction ≈ $476M/yr in averted productivity loss.

Community cancer screening equity

$6.18B pool

Detroit cancer mortality runs above Ann Arbor's. Navigation + AI-triaged screening reminders close detection gaps in underserved areas.
